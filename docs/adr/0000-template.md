# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by `NNNN` (link the record that replaced this one)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is true that forces a decision now? Constraints, pressures, the thing that
stopped working. Facts, not opinions — if a reader disagrees with this section
they will disagree with the decision, and that is the useful conversation.

## Decision

What we are doing. Present tense, active voice: "We use X." One paragraph.

## Alternatives considered

The options that were genuinely on the table, and the specific reason each lost.
A list with no rejected alternative means no decision was made.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we have now committed to.
Name the cost explicitly — a decision with no downsides was not a decision.
