## Copy voice — plain German, `du` everywhere (shipped 2026-08-20, evening)

**Change class: Standard.** Copy-only, but sweeping and it moves glossary terms,
so it was taken up a class rather than run as Trivial.

**User correction, in their words:** *"mir wirken sie irgendwie zu hochgestochen.
Weil irgendwie soll die App ja alle Menschen ansprechen aber die Sprache ist sehr
hoch."* Four causes were found, in order of effect:

1. **The address was split.** 168 `Sie` strings vs. 32 `du` — and the split ran
   right across the sign-up boundary: the landing page said *"Du suchst aus"*,
   the app two clicks later said *"Alles zu Ihrem Wortschatz"*. `profile` alone
   contained both. No single change introduced this; it drifted, because nothing
   owned it.
2. **Research vocabulary reached the surface.** Lemma, Paradigmenzelle,
   Paradigmata-Tableau (not a German word), Frequenzband, Signal, Niveaumodell,
   Lexikalische Abdeckung, Formbeherrschung, Erinnerungsstabilität.
3. **The register was essayistic.** *"der Nenner, den niemand in dieser Kategorie
   veröffentlicht"*, *"ist Arithmetik, kein Versagen"*, *"Echte Wahl ohne
   Entscheidungslast"*. 19 sentences ran past 22 words.
4. **Machine-translation damage**, which read as stiffness rather than as bugs:
   *"Wie viele Starter-Lemmas Sie an die Bedeutung erinnern"* (ungrammatical),
   *"mindestens zwei Abstände in der Wiederholung"* ("two spaced successes"),
   `Abstands-Wiederholungssitzung`.

### Owner decisions

| Question | Decision | Rules out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Address | `du`, lowercase, everywhere | mixed surfaces; "formal for account pages" |
| Depth | core surfaces now; catalogue vocabulary later | a single sweep over all ~60 method entries |
| Technical terms | everyday word on the surface, term inside the disclosure | dropping the terms (loses checkability) and leaving them exposed |
| Locales | English moved with German | letting the source locale drift from the one people read |
