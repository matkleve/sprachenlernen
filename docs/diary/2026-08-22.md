## Breakthrough — photographic wood FFT at native crack scale (on main)

Owner reaction on the sharp wood-01 remake: *"Omg this is so close"* — treated as
a **major breakthrough** for progression Workshop materials.

**Root cause of bubbly cracks:** `FEATURE_SCALE=9.0` blew the crack spectrum ~9×
wider in pixel space. Heeger–Bergen + `SPEC_SMOOTH_SIGMA` soften edges; at scale
9 the result is fat dark blobs with puffy rim halos, not fine checking lines.

**Fix on main:** `FEATURE_SCALE` default **9.0 → 1.0**. Reference:
`design/progression/breakthrough-wood-01-final.png`.
