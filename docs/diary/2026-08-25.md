# 2026-08-25 — Why no method except cards feeds a signal

**Change class: Standard** (study only — no code). Owner: *"the whole system is
that the system keeps track of your different skills across all methods."* It
does not. Six built engines write nothing a signal can read.

## Study

[STUDY-056](../study/STUDY-056-skill-evidence-from-every-method.md) — skill
evidence: what a method may claim, and how strong.

Seven findings, the first two of which were wording accidents rather than open
research questions:

- **S1** `targetSignal` is the **evaluation** channel from
  [STUDY-024](../study/STUDY-024-readiness-and-difficulty.md) tier 2 (*"judged on
  a narrow target signal"*) — it was read as the emission list. That conflation is
  why input methods record nothing. One evaluation signal, many observations.
- **S2** Listening needs no eighth signal. [STUDY-003](../study/STUDY-003-level-model.md)
  wrote coverage as predicting *reading* comprehension; E4 tabulates coverage
  bands **per modality** (95 % listening ≈ 2–3k families vs reading ≈ 4–5k).
  Missing dimension: **modality on the observation**.
- **S3** Owner's proposal — untapped word = evidence of knowing it — holds, at
  **low strength**. E3: recognition overstates recall ~20 %, and context supplies
  more support than a card does. R9 bars the crossing to production. Plus: not
  tapping and not noticing are indistinguishable, so it needs a companion
  observation that the text was actually read.
- **S4** No strength / sample-size field ⇒ aggregation undefined. R6's
  standard-error discipline applies to cross-method pooling.
- **S5** Admission test: *could a learner produce this number without doing the
  language?* Minutes read and words seen fail it (C3 counts that only rise).
- **S6** An encounter is not a review. Writing `good` for an untapped word claims
  a retrieval nobody performed **and stretches the interval** — the app would
  schedule less practice for skimming. Writing `again` for a tap invents a lapse
  and corrupts FSRS difficulty.
- **S7** Preview coverage describes the material; evidence coverage describes the
  session. Same function, not the same fact.

## The actual gap

`readLevel` in `lib/level-model.ts` takes `Task[]`. Not "mostly card data" —
**structurally card-only**, so four of seven layer-1 signals are hardcoded
`no-data` and `SIGNALS_FEEDING.listening` is `[]`. Extensive reading computes
lexical coverage at composition time and throws it away.
`docs/specs/page/progress.md` states *only card-engine Reviews feed this page*,
and `method-engines.md` lists exercise-runner writes as *"Session log (future)"* —
so this is a written-down design decision, not drift.
