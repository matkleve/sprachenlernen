## The correction I had to make to my own first pass

Morning framing was "only card reviews feed the level model". Wrong emphasis.
Reading `lib/level-model.ts` against `data/methods/*.json`:

`readSignals` computes **vocabulary size, form mastery, recall stability** from
`Task[]`. `SIGNALS_FEEDING` routes reading ← lexical coverage, speaking ←
production quality, writing ← production quality, listening ← nothing.

**The two sets are disjoint.** Not thin — empty intersection. Every skill reads
`not-measured` for every learner regardless of input, and `overall` is permanently
`withheld`. And `lib/level-model.test.ts` asserts exactly that, on purpose:

> recall stability feeds no skill — so a learner who has reviewed for weeks has
> still not been measured on reading, listening, speaking or writing.

So the honesty machinery is **finished and correct**; the evidence supply is
empty. The work is upstream of the level model, and the tempting shortcut —
route recall stability into reading so the page moves — is precisely what that
test exists to prevent. Recorded as `S1` because I nearly proposed it.

## Study

[STUDY-056](../study/STUDY-056-skill-evidence-from-every-method.md), rewritten
from the morning's reading-only version to cover all 59 methods and content
reuse. Twelve findings; the ones that moved my understanding:

- **S3** No spec contains a signal-to-skill table. `SIGNALS_FEEDING` was inferred
  from two prose fragments in STUDY-003's layer-1 list. `listening: []` because
  **no sentence in the chapter mentions listening.** An absence of prose,
  hardened into a data structure and then tested.
- **S4** Production quality feeds speaking *and* writing; 9 methods target it,
  7 write-only vs 2 speak-only. First writing method to emit makes a learner who
  has never spoken read **speaking: measured**. Latent, with a deadline.
- **S7** Owner's content-reuse idea (article → sentences → words) is not thrift,
  it is the fix for S6. Reading is wide+weak and by E1 not retrieval; dictation
  on the *same* sentences is narrow+strong and is; carding makes it durable. E2
  makes it spaced. `material-unit.md` already resolves one Source at
  `sentence`/`paragraph`/`full` — **the plumbing exists, the memory does not**,
  so nothing can choose yesterday's article on purpose.
- **S11** 18 unhosted methods have no signal honestly (outside the app, two
  ledgers). 9 hosted ones are real gaps. **Minimal pairs** is hosted, declares
  listening, and is forced-choice discrimination — cheapest objective listening
  measurement in the catalogue, no audio pipeline needed.
- **S6/S9 reverse STUDY-055 on one point.** I argued for deleting *Durchsehen*.
  Tap evidence is uninterpretable without evidence the text was read at all —
  not tapping and not noticing are indistinguishable. Keep one check, change its
  job to admissibility gate. Same guard as anti-gaming.
