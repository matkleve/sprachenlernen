## Method badge UX study (T-B10c queued)

Owner design review of methods pages: flat grey layout, two-line evidence/intensity
pills with full `rounded-pill` cutting off text, shell title truncating long names
("Background listening wit…") with no in-page hero.

**Decision:** three badge families — skill contribution, evidence grade, effort
dots — not one global copper/silver/gold per method (violates UC-046 single-score
ranking; conflates orthogonal facts per study/12 two ledgers).

Owner's metal metaphor scoped to **per-skill contribution level** only, and not
shown as metal words in UI — use skill-colored marks with primary/secondary/slight.
