## Review horizon UX study (T-W16)

Owner + agent design review of `/words` horizon: GitHub-style past calendar
rejected; forward 4-week tile columns preferred; routine users should not see a
prominent chart; FSRS shows deterministic `due` bins (not retrievability dice);
overdue tasks excluded from bins; causal line is the real UX lever (study/04 G2).

triggers (gap ≥ 7d, bulk add ≥ 30/48h, peak week, first week) or manual expand;

pages (`/methods`, `/words`, `/progress`, etc.).
