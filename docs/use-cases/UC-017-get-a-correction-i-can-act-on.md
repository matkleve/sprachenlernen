# UC-017 — Write something and get a correction I can act on

<!-- id: UC-017 -->
<!-- specs: SPEC-service-sentence-check -->

**Who:** a learner who wants to produce language but not in real time.
**Wants to:** write a few sentences and see exactly what was wrong and why.
**So that:** they build production ability without the pressure of speaking, and
can see which kinds of error are disappearing.

Derived from [`../study/STUDY-006-production.md`](../study/STUDY-006-production.md),
"Schreiben".

## Today

Correction arrives either as a rewritten paragraph — useful to read, impossible
to learn from, because the learner cannot tell what changed or why — or as a
grade. Neither accumulates: nothing tells you whether the mistake you made today
is the same one you made in March.

## Success looks like

- Formats that take under five minutes: a sentence using a target word, three
  lines of diary, a description of a picture, a summary of something just read.
- Corrections are shown as a **difference**, change by change, not as a rewritten
  text.
- Every change carries a **category** from a fixed list — grammar, word choice,
  idiom, style — because only categorised corrections can be counted over time.
- Style suggestions are marked as alternatives, never as errors, and do not
  appear below B1.
- The categories accumulate into a trend the learner can see: which kind of
  mistake is fading, which is not (UC-004).
- Recurring errors become cards, without the learner asking.
- Writing is available as a way to answer a card, not only as its own exercise.

## Out of scope

Long-form essays, exam-style marking, scoring against a rubric, and correction
by other humans.
