# UC-034 — Find out exactly what is missing between me and this episode

<!-- id: UC-034 -->
<!-- specs: SPEC-service-coverage, SPEC-feature-content-gap, SPEC-feature-content-traceability -->

**Who:** a learner who has a specific thing they want to understand — an episode,
an article, a song.
**Wants to:** know precisely which words stand between them and it, and learn
those.
**So that:** vocabulary work has a destination instead of being open-ended.

Derived from
[`../study/STUDY-017-milestones-and-map.md`](../study/STUDY-017-milestones-and-map.md),
K3.

## Today

The learner is told to learn vocabulary, and separately finds that content is
too hard. Nothing joins the two. The gap between "I know 2,300 words" and "I can
follow this programme" is never made explicit, so neither number motivates.

## Success looks like

- For any saved item, the app states current coverage and **lists the specific
  words** that would bring it into the comfortable band.
- Those words can be taken as a set, scheduled ahead of other new words, with an
  estimate of how long it will take.
- The estimate is based on the learner's own recent pace and shows its
  uncertainty.
- When the set is done, the app says so and offers the item — the payoff is
  experienced, not reported.
- Words in such a set are ordinary cards afterwards: they keep their schedule and
  count normally. Nothing about this creates a parallel system.
- If an item needs far more than a reasonable set, the app says that plainly and
  suggests something closer instead of producing a list of 400 words.

## Out of scope

Guaranteeing comprehension after learning the set — coverage predicts
understanding, it does not deliver it, and the wording must not promise more.
