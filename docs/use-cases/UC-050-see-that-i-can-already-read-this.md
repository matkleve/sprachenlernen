# UC-050 — See for myself that I can already read this

<!-- id: UC-050 -->
<!-- specs: SPEC-page-method-menu, SPEC-feature-demonstration-sentence -->

**Who:** anyone opening the app, on any day.
**Wants to:** see one real sentence in the language and find out, on the spot,
whether they can read it.
**So that:** the level stops being a number somebody computed and becomes
something they just did.

Derived from
[`../study/24-speaking-as-the-goal.md`](../study/24-speaking-as-the-goal.md) S2.

## Today

Apps announce progress: a percentage, a crown, a streak. None of it is
verifiable from the inside, so it is either believed or ignored. Where a level is
claimed at all, it is usually self-assessed — and self-assessment is
systematically wrong in a known direction: lower-proficiency learners
overestimate, advanced learners underestimate.

## Success looks like

- One sentence in the target language on the home surface, chosen **slightly
  above** the current estimate — at level it confirms, above level it informs.
- The learner is asked to **tap the words they are unsure of**, not asked whether
  they understood. Understanding is demonstrated, never self-reported.
- Nothing tapped and confirmed → the app names what that shows: *"that was a B1.2
  sentence."* Words tapped → *"not yet — that one is B1.4"*, with no failure
  framing.
- A tapped word can become a card in one further tap, and is otherwise forgotten.
- The response feeds the level estimate as one item among many
  ([UC-004](UC-004-know-where-i-stand.md)) — never as a verdict from a single
  sentence.
- Where the language has no level-labelled sentences, the sentence is still shown
  by coverage, and **no level label is attached to it**
  ([UC-036](UC-036-know-how-much-to-trust-this-language.md)).
- Ignoring it entirely costs nothing. It is not a gate to the rest of the app.

## Out of scope

A daily quiz, a streak on it, several sentences a day, and any score derived from
one sentence alone.
