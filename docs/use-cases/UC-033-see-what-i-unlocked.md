# UC-033 — See what my learning has unlocked

<!-- id: UC-033 -->
<!-- specs: SPEC-feature-content-traceability, SPEC-service-coverage -->

**Who:** a learner who has put in a month and wants to know what it bought.
**Wants to:** see the content that has moved from "too hard" into reach.
**So that:** progress is something they can go and experience, not a number they
have to trust.

Derived from
[`../study/STUDY-017-milestones-and-map.md`](../study/STUDY-017-milestones-and-map.md)
and [`../study/STUDY-008-motivation.md`](../study/STUDY-008-motivation.md) M5.

## Today

Progress is asserted — a level, a streak, a chart. Nothing shows the learner the
thing they can now do that they could not do before, so the claim stays abstract
and has to be believed rather than checked.

## Success looks like

- The app recomputes coverage for everything the learner has saved, and reports
  what crossed into the comfortable band: "four episodes and nine texts moved
  from demanding to comfortable this month".
- Each item names its before and after coverage, with dates.
- Something the learner abandoned earlier is offered back at the right moment —
  the strongest form of this, because they remember failing at it.
- Historical coverage is stored with a timestamp **and** the calibration in force
  at the time, so a recalibration can never manufacture progress that did not
  happen.
- The reverse also shows honestly: after a long break, some items move back out
  of reach, and the app says so without drama and with the way back.

## Out of scope

Notifying about every small change, a feed of unlock events, and unlocks for
content the learner never expressed interest in.
