# UC-009 — Practise on paper, and have it count

<!-- id: UC-009 -->
<!-- specs:  -->

**Who:** a learner who wants to write by hand, take dictation, or drill forms
without a screen.
**Wants to:** do a real exercise on paper and have the result feed back into
what the app schedules next.
**So that:** the most demanding kinds of practice are not excluded just because
they do not happen in an app.

Derived from [`../study/STUDY-007-offline-and-paper.md`](../study/STUDY-007-offline-and-paper.md).

## Today

Paper exercises exist as printable worksheets that know nothing about the
learner and receive nothing back. Doing one is a detour from the app rather than
part of it, so it stops after two weeks.

## Success looks like

- An exercise can be generated from **this learner's own material** — dictation
  sentences built from their known words plus the words whose spoken form is
  still weak; form tables built from the endings they actually got wrong.
- Dictation is read three times (normal, slow with pauses, normal), controllable
  by the same commands as UC-008.
- Printable output is A4, black and white, readable without colour, with the
  answer key on a separable page.
- Afterwards the learner checks their own work against the key and marks what
  was wrong; those items become cards.
- Form drills mix regular and irregular items rather than blocking by pattern.
- An exercise that is never returned produces **no backlog and no reminder**.
  Nothing about this may recreate the problem UC-006 exists to prevent.

## Out of scope

Photographing handwritten answers for automatic marking, handwriting recognition
of any kind, and grading of free written text (that belongs with writing
feedback, not here).
