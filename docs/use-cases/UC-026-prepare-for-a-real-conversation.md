# UC-026 — Prepare for a conversation that is actually happening

<!-- id: UC-026 -->
<!-- specs:  -->

**Who:** someone with a doctor's appointment, a viewing, an office visit, a
tandem meeting or a job interview in the target language, on a known date.
**Wants to:** walk in with the thirty words and ten phrases that will come up.
**So that:** the highest-stakes real use of the language is the one they were
actually prepared for.

## Today

Nothing prepares for a specific event. Learners either go in unprepared or build
their own list from a dictionary the night before — which is exactly the right
instinct and exactly the wrong tool, because the words are met once and never
again. Afterwards, everything they could not say is forgotten by the next day.

## Success looks like

- The learner names the situation and the date. The app assembles the vocabulary
  and phrases that situation actually needs, at their level.
- The material is front-loaded into the days before, so it is reviewed rather
  than crammed — the schedule is told there is a deadline.
- It is available as a printable sheet and as an audio run-through, because the
  ten minutes before an appointment involve neither a quiet room nor a free
  screen.
- Optionally, a rehearsal of the situation with the conversation partner
  (UC-015).
- **Afterwards**, one question: what could you not say? Three lines typed in
  become cards. This is the single highest-value card source in the product,
  because the need is proven rather than predicted.
- If the event passes without follow-up, nothing nags and nothing accumulates.

## Out of scope

Calendar integration, real-time translation during the event, and treating this
as a substitute for general progress.
