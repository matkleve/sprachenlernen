# UC-039 — See what today's session consists of before starting it

<!-- id: UC-039 -->
<!-- specs:  -->

**Who:** anyone opening the app with a fixed amount of time.
**Wants to:** know what the next fifteen minutes contain before committing to
them.
**So that:** starting is a decision rather than a leap, and the session can be
matched to the time and attention actually available.

Derived from [`../studie/01-duolingo.md`](../studie/01-duolingo.md) S2,
[`../studie/12-methodenkarten.md`](../studie/12-methodenkarten.md) and
[`../studie/04-karteikarten-srs.md`](../studie/04-karteikarten-srs.md).

## Today

Sessions start opaque: an unknown number of items of unknown kinds. The learner
cannot tell whether this is five minutes or forty, so they either avoid starting
when time is short or abandon halfway — and an abandoned session teaches the
habit that sessions get abandoned.

## Success looks like

- Before starting: how long, how many items, what kinds, and roughly how
  demanding.
- The composition is explained in one line — how many are due reviews, how many
  are new, what drove the mix.
- The learner can change the length before starting, and the app re-plans rather
  than truncating: the most urgent items stay in.
- Progress within the session is visible and the end is reachable. The count
  never grows while the learner is working through it.
- Interrupting mid-session loses nothing, and the remainder is folded into the
  next one silently.
- Nowhere does this surface show an outstanding total (UC-006).

## Out of scope

Letting the learner hand-pick individual cards, and building a session from
scratch by hand.
