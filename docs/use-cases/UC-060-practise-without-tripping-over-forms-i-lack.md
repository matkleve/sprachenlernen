# UC-060 — Practise without tripping over forms I have not got yet

<!-- id: UC-060 -->
<!-- specs:  -->

**Who:** a learner whose vocabulary is well ahead of their inflection — the
normal case in Spanish and Italian.
**Wants to:** work on reading, writing or correction without every third item
turning into an unrelated grammar problem.
**So that:** a session trains the thing it claims to train.

Derived from
[`../study/03-level-model.md`](../study/03-level-model.md) ("the second axis")
and [`../study/05-input-reading-listening.md`](../study/05-input-reading-listening.md)
(the 95–98 % bands). This is the mechanism that answers *"if someone is not as
good at the plural of certain words you can't give him those"* — and it is
**material selection inside a running method**, not a readiness state. UC-057 is
the other answer to the same worry; confusing them produces a lock where a filter
belongs.

## Today

Difficulty is chosen by vocabulary alone, because vocabulary is what apps count.
So a text at nominal 95 % coverage is full of tenses the learner has never
produced, and the failure gets attributed to the words. The learner cannot tell
which of the two failed, and neither can the app.

## Success looks like

- Material selection considers the **paradigm cells** an item contains, not only
  its lemmas — the cell is already carried on every form the lexicon resolves.
- Selection excludes only what cannot be **attempted**, never what would be
  failed. An item the learner can attempt and get wrong is the valuable case:
  errorful retrieval with feedback outperforms study alone, and confidently-held
  errors are the best corrected of all
  ([26](../study/26-readiness-and-difficulty.md) R4). The line E3 draws is "no
  chance of *retrieval*", not "no chance of success".
- When the method's purpose is form mastery, the weak cells are the **target**
  rather than the exclusion (UC-041).
- Density of unheld forms is a **dial with a purpose behind it**, not a value to
  minimise. A method that exists to create learning opportunities runs at the
  lower band even though it feels worse
  ([26](../study/26-readiness-and-difficulty.md) R5).
- The learner is never shown a text or task engineered so heavily that it stops
  resembling the language. Where that is the only way to hit the band, the app
  says the band cannot be hit rather than faking it.
- Difficulty within a method has **one** documented source of truth per method —
  which quantity, which band, which fallback — so two methods cannot disagree
  about the same learner.
- When a session was harder than intended, the app can say which of the two
  causes it was: unknown words or unheld forms. That distinction is the whole
  point of having two signals.
- A language whose profile declares little inflection simply does not apply this
  dimension. It is not an empty filter that quietly matches nothing.

## Out of scope

Rewriting or simplifying texts to fit (UC-030), grammar as a curriculum of
topics, and any single global difficulty slider — difficulty is per method and
per signal, or it is meaningless.

## Undecided

- **⚠ SPEC GAP: does coverage count lemmas or forms?** The reading bands are
  stated over "known words"; the two readings give materially different texts,
  and every calibration downstream inherits the choice.
- **⚠ SPEC GAP: no normative table of difficulty parameters exists.** Reading has
  bands; correction, writing and dictation have dials with no bands. Until one
  table names the quantity and band per method, each implementation will invent
  its own.
