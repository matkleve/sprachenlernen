# UC-055 — Use the language for something real this week

<!-- id: UC-055 -->
<!-- specs:  -->

**Who:** someone whose practice is going well and who has still never used the
language for a purpose they would have had anyway.
**Wants to:** do the actual thing once — read something they wanted to read, say
something to a person, write a message with a recipient.
**So that:** the progress they are told about becomes something they have
experienced, not something they have been informed of.

Derived from
[`../study/STUDY-023-why-it-does-not-feel-productive.md`](../study/STUDY-023-why-it-does-not-feel-productive.md)
C2 and P5.

## Today

Every app practises pieces: an item, a sentence, a lesson, a card. Almost none
asks the learner to perform the task. That absence is not only a didactic gap —
performance accomplishments are the strongest single source of self-efficacy, so a
product that never lets you perform withholds the main input of the belief that
predicts whether you continue. This is why diligent practice can feel like
nothing is happening: in that case the feeling is correct.

## Success looks like

- At most one real-use suggestion is live at a time, and it is concrete enough to
  act on today: *this* article, *this* person, *this* message — not "try speaking
  more".
- It is chosen against what the learner can currently do, so it is small enough
  to succeed. Hard is allowed; too hard achieves nothing.
- Doing it is **not tracked as a session, scored, or streaked**. Reporting back is
  optional, marked as self-reported, and never feeds a level-1 signal.
- Declining is a complete outcome, and declining repeatedly does not escalate.
- Afterwards the app can name what it demonstrated in the learner's own terms —
  and says plainly when it cannot measure what happened.
- The learner can see that this is deliberately the one part of the product that
  is not measured, and why.

## Out of scope

Matching learners with each other, hosting the conversation, verifying that the
task was done, and any consequence for not doing it.
