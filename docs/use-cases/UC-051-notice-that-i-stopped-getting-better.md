# UC-051 — Notice that I have stopped getting better

<!-- id: UC-051 -->
<!-- specs:  -->

**Who:** someone who has been practising steadily for months, mostly in the ways
they find comfortable.
**Wants to:** be told when the numbers have stopped moving, and what specifically
to do about it.
**So that:** they do not spend a year confirming what they already know.

Derived from
[`../study/24-speaking-as-the-goal.md`](../study/24-speaking-as-the-goal.md) S3.

## Today

Nothing notices. An app whose reward is the daily return has no reason to say
"this is not working" — and the learner cannot see it either, because activity
feels like progress. The plateau is the best-documented failure mode in
second-language learning and the least-supported one in software.

## Success looks like

- The app watches four markers it already has data for: steady below-norm
  accuracy on one structure, **alternation** between right and wrong use of the
  *same* structure, backsliding on cards that were mature, and a flattening trend
  per skill.
- What is said is an **observation, not a verdict**: *"ser and estar have been
  alternating for six weeks — about as often right as wrong. That is not a memory
  problem, it is a distinction problem."*
- The suggestion addresses that specific marker — alternation → contrast
  practice; a flat curve → a different kind of session — never "practise more".
- **The no-data case is stated plainly and is the most important one:** a learner
  who has never spoken has no speaking level, and the app says so once rather
  than showing a low number.
- It obeys the same cap as every other prompt: at most one a day, in the same
  place as the daily method choice
  ([UC-010](UC-010-choose-how-to-practise-today.md)), and dismissible.
- Every claim opens into the data it came from
  ([UC-004](UC-004-know-where-i-stand.md)). No hint without a derivation.

## Out of scope

Push notifications, motivational framing, comparison with other learners, and
any judgement about how much the person practises.
