# UC-061 — Be told what to learn first, without being told what to do

<!-- id: UC-061 -->
<!-- specs:  -->

**Who:** a learner who has looked at the catalogue, believes all of it, and does
not know where to start.
**Wants to:** a recommended order, with the reason attached.
**So that:** freedom of choice does not become the burden of choosing well with
no information.

Derived from [`../study/02-evidence.md`](../study/02-evidence.md) E7 (choice
supports motivation, but beginners are overwhelmed by too much of it) and E10
(learners taught *how* to use a method benefit measurably more). Bounded by
[`../study/10-antipatterns.md`](../study/10-antipatterns.md) A8: a required
station before practice is forbidden, so an order may be recommended and never
enforced.

## Today

Two failures, symmetrical. Curriculum apps impose an order and the learner never
finds out why, so they cannot deviate intelligently. Tool-shaped apps impose
nothing and the learner picks by vibe — which
[`../study/02-evidence.md`](../study/02-evidence.md) E13 says is systematically
the least useful choice.

## Success looks like

- Where an order genuinely matters, the app states it as a recommendation with a
  reason: "this one goes better after you have some plurals, because half its
  items are plural."
- The reason is about the **material**, not about the learner's worth or
  readiness to be trusted.
- The recommendation is one line on the card. It is not a step, not a stage, not
  a path, and it is never the only route to the method.
- Ignoring it costs nothing and is not remembered as a deviation.
- Where an order does **not** matter, the app says nothing. Inventing an order
  for methods that are genuinely parallel is worse than silence, because it
  teaches the learner that the orders are arbitrary and they stop reading them.
- The app distinguishes "this is a prerequisite of the material" from "this is
  what we would do next" — the first is a fact, the second is advice, and
  conflating them makes both untrustworthy.

## Out of scope

Lessons, units, paths, stages, trees, and any sequencing the learner cannot step
out of. Also: ordering the *catalogue* — the daily menu already has its own
composition rules ([12](../study/12-method-cards.md)) and this must not become a
second, competing sort.

## Undecided

- **⚠ SPEC GAP: where recommended order is authored.** Per method as a static
  hint in the catalogue data, or derived from the learner's holdings? A static
  hint is honest and cheap but cannot say "for you"; a derived one can, and needs
  the quantity UC-057 has not chosen yet.
