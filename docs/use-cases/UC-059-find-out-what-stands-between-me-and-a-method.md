# UC-059 — Find out what stands between me and a method I want

<!-- id: UC-059 -->
<!-- specs: SPEC-service-coverage, SPEC-feature-content-gap -->

**Who:** a learner who wants to do extensive reading, or mixed paradigm tables,
and the app has nothing it can build for them yet.
**Wants to:** see exactly how far off it is, in words rather than in levels.
**So that:** "not yet" becomes a distance they can close instead of a door.

Derived from
[`../study/STUDY-017-milestones-and-map.md`](../study/STUDY-017-milestones-and-map.md) K3 —
the coverage calculator read backwards — and the **no material yet** state in
UC-057. UC-034 does this for a **content item** the learner chose; this is the
same arithmetic aimed at a **method**, and the two must produce the same numbers
or one of them is wrong.

## Today

Nothing. Either the exercise runs badly on unsuitable material, or the method is
absent with no explanation, and the learner concludes the app does not have it.
The one thing never said is the useful thing: how close they are.

## Success looks like

- The method is visible with what is missing stated concretely: "extensive
  reading needs a text where you know about 95 % of the words. Your best text
  sits at 81 % — roughly 300 more of the top 2,000 words."
- A time estimate from the learner's **own** recent pace, with its uncertainty
  attached, never a flat "4 days".
- The route is one tap, and it is an existing mechanism rather than a new one:
  the missing set becomes ordinary scheduled cards, keeping their normal
  schedule and counting normally (UC-034).
- Tapping the method opens this explanation. It does **not** start a session,
  because there is no session to start — and the copy says that plainly rather
  than implying permission is being withheld.
- When the gap closes, the app says so and offers the method — the payoff is
  experienced rather than announced (UC-033).
- If the gap is very large, the app says so plainly and names something closer,
  rather than producing a list of 400 words (UC-034).
- The state can go **backwards** after a long pause, and says so without drama,
  with the way back beside it — honesty rule 1 in
  [`../study/STUDY-003-level-model.md`](../study/STUDY-003-level-model.md).

## Out of scope

Promising that the method will feel good once the gap closes; readiness for
off-app methods, which have no material for the app to build; and generating
easier material to manufacture readiness — that is UC-030's territory and a
different decision.

## Undecided

- **⚠ SPEC GAP: whether "no material yet" is visible or absent.** UC-057 decides
  it is visible, on the grounds that the distance is the most motivating thing on
  the surface. But the context filter removes unperformable methods entirely,
  "not even greyed out" ([12](../study/STUDY-010-method-cards.md)), and two adjacent
  states behaving oppositely needs stating as a decision rather than inheriting
  from whichever code path was written first.
