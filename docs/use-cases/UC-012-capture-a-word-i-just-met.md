# UC-012 — Turn a word I just met into practice

<!-- id: UC-012 -->
<!-- specs:  -->

**Who:** anyone who just hit a word they did not know — in a text, in audio, in
a conversation, on a form at the town hall.
**Wants to:** capture it in two seconds and never think about it again until the
app asks.
**So that:** the words they actually needed are the words they end up knowing.

Derived from [`../studie/04-karteikarten-srs.md`](../studie/04-karteikarten-srs.md),
"Wo Karten herkommen".

## Today

Capturing costs more than the word is worth. Writing a good card is a skill —
one meaning or five, with context or without, which direction — and learners
either make bad cards, make none, or abandon the app that demanded them. Words
met outside the app are lost entirely.

## Success looks like

- One action captures a word from anywhere the app already shows text or plays
  audio: reading (UC-007), listening (UC-008), a correction after speaking or
  writing, a dictation mistake (UC-009).
- The captured card carries **the sentence it came from**, not just the word.
- A word can be typed in from outside the app in one field, and the app supplies
  the rest — lemma, meaning in that context, audio, frequency rank.
- The learner is never required to choose a card type. The staged types
  (recognition → meaning recall → form recall → audio recall) follow from the
  scheduler.
- Capturing something already known says so, and does not create a duplicate.
- Tapping part of a fixed expression offers **the whole expression**, not the
  single word.
- Capture never interrupts what the learner was doing. Reading continues;
  audio does not stop.

## Out of scope

Bulk import from other apps, sharing decks with other people, and editing card
internals.
