# UC-041 — Know the forms, not just the words

<!-- id: UC-041 -->
<!-- specs: SPEC-service-form-practice -->

**Who:** anyone learning a language where words change shape — which is most of
them, and certainly Spanish and Italian.
**Wants to:** find out which inflection patterns they can actually produce, and
practise the ones they cannot.
**So that:** "I know 2,000 words" stops meaning "I can read" and starts meaning
something they can also say.

Derived from
[`../study/03-level-model.md`](../study/03-level-model.md), "Die zweite
Achse", and [`../study/07-offline-and-paper.md`](../study/07-offline-and-paper.md) Ü3.

## Today

Vocabulary apps count dictionary forms and quietly assume the rest. But knowing
*parlare* does not mean producing *parliamo*; the three Italian conjugation
classes diverge, *-ire* splits again, and the most frequent verbs are the
irregular ones. So a learner reads comfortably, cannot speak, and every number
in the app says they are fine.

Grammar apps have the opposite failure: they teach patterns as topics,
disconnected from the words the learner actually has.

## Success looks like

- Every practice item is tagged with **where its form sits in the paradigm** —
  class, tense/mood, person for verbs; number and gender for nouns and
  adjectives.
- Failing a form while knowing its dictionary word is reported as a **form gap**,
  not as a vocabulary gap, and the two are never mixed in one figure.
- The learner can see which patterns are solid and which are not — including
  that the answer differs per pattern, and per word inside an irregular one.
- Form mastery contributes to the level as its own signal. A large vocabulary
  with weak forms cannot produce a high speaking or writing level.
- Practice for a gap uses the learner's **own** words, not example verbs, and
  mixes classes rather than drilling one pattern in a block.
- Irregular high-frequency words are treated individually, because that is where
  a class rule stops predicting anything.
- A language whose profile declares little or no inflection simply does not show
  this dimension. It is not an empty section.

## Out of scope

Grammar as a curriculum of topics, terminology as a subject, and generating full
paradigm tables for words the learner has never met.
