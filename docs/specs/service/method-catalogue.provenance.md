# Method catalogue — provenance

Split child of [`method-catalogue.md`](method-catalogue.md). The parent owns
scope, behavior and data; this file records where each field's value came from
and every place two chapters had to be reconciled.

It exists because "transcribed from the study" is a claim that decays. Fields
get filled in, defaults get chosen under time pressure, and a year later nobody
can tell which numbers have a source. Disagreeing with an authored value should
cost an edit, and finding out that it *is* authored should cost one lookup.

## Transcribed

From [21](../../study/21-method-catalogue-and-context.md): the entries
themselves, their section, `trains`, `evidence`, `demanding`, and the context
requirements. From [12](../../study/12-method-cards.md): the five floors. From
[24](../../study/24-speaking-as-the-goal.md): the commitments.

Chapter 21's seven tables hold 55 rows. Minus the three entries moved to
commitments (reconciliation 3) that is 52, plus free production is 53 methods.
No row was dropped, merged or renamed past recognition.

## Authored

`intensity`, `durations`, `doesNotDo`, `targetSignal`, `hosted`, `skills` and
`reviewAfterDays`. None is derivable from the study; all are needed before a
card can be rendered.

`skills` deserves its own line because it looks transcribed and is not: chapter
21 has a prose *Trains* column, not a skill set, so every entry's skills are a
reading of that prose. Four entries carry an empty set — the three
vocabulary methods and *label the flat* — because `SKILLS` has no `vocabulary`
value. See the parent's Open.

Requirements were **widened** beyond the chapter in six places, each because the
chapter's list was plainly partial rather than restrictive: partial dictation
gains paper, dictogloss gains keyboard, cloze sentences and photo captioning
gain keyboard, background listening gains sound (it has to require something —
the chapter says "any"), and reading aloud gains eyes-free, since the text has
to be visible. The last one had a consequence worth recording: an early test
used reading aloud as its example of a chapter-given eyes-free rule, so it was
asserting an authored requirement while appearing to assert a transcribed one.
It now uses extensive reading, whose eyes-free requirement is in the chapter.

## Reconciliations

Six places where two chapters disagreed, or where the schema could hold only one
of two values. Listed rather than performed quietly.

1. **Free production** carries a floor in [12](../../study/12-method-cards.md)
   and appears in none of [21](../../study/21-method-catalogue-and-context.md)'s
   tables. Added to *Writing*, since [06](../../study/06-production.md) treats it
   as a method and a floor presupposes one.
2. **Its evidence grade is authored too**, and is the only grade in the
   catalogue with no row behind it. Set to **B**, the strongest mark
   [06](../../study/06-production.md) gives. This matters more than it looks:
   evidence grade is normative for menu ranking until an effect estimate exists,
   so an invented A would have ranked the one sourceless entry above fifty-two
   sourced ones.
3. **The dictation floor** (1× / 10 days) attaches to full dictation on paper,
   which is what [07](../../study/07-offline-and-paper.md) describes and what
   [12](../../study/12-method-cards.md)'s own card illustration implies
   ("headphones + pen & paper"). Partial dictation carries none.
4. **Switch your phone's language**, **label the flat** and **pursue a hobby**
   are methods in [21](../../study/21-method-catalogue-and-context.md) and meet
   [24](../../study/24-speaking-as-the-goal.md)'s definition of a commitment.
   They are commitments, and appear once.
5. **Background listening** is marked "C, weak" in
   [21](../../study/21-method-catalogue-and-context.md). The grade is `C`; the
   "weak" half lives in its `doesNotDo`. It was briefly `D`, which is the worst
   entry in the catalogue to shift by hand — the chapter singles it out as the
   test of whether weak methods are included honestly.
6. **Writing and performing a play** is marked "B/C". The schema admits one
   grade, so it is `C`. Collapsing a range is a decision, not a transcription.

## Where a value was refused rather than invented

- No floor was added to fill the column. Five methods have one; forty-eight do
  not.
- No paradigm-style default was applied to `targetSignal`. Twenty-one methods
  have none because no layer-1 signal fits, not because nobody looked.
- The kitchen contradiction (parent, Open) was left standing. Chapter 21 gives
  cooking from a recipe the context "kitchen" and defines the kitchen as eyes
  and hands gone; reading a recipe needs eyes. Either value would have been a
  rule nobody decided.
